Our Vision

Your Dental Clinic in Birmingham


We offer a wide range of cosmetic and restorative treatments to refresh, restore and rejuvenate your smile, with our clients' needs at the centre of everything we do.

We partner with Ora Dental at their purpose-built clinic in the Edgbaston Medical Quarter, which has an on-site dental laboratory so treatment can often be completed in one visit.

Please note: Edgbaston Smile does not admit NHS patients. Only one person is allowed in the clinic for your appointment.

Dental Treatments

A full range of services including cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, dental implants and general dental treatments.

Cosmetic Dentistry

  • VeneersPorcelain or composite veneers to transform your smile.
  • Composite BondingResin applied to the surface of your teeth.
  • Teeth WhiteningGuaranteed results from expert dentists.

General Dentistry

  • White FillingsNatural, tooth-coloured resin fillings.

  • Air PolishingGently removes stains and plaque.

  • Dental Implant BridgesFor 2 to 4 adjacent missing teeth.
  • All-on-Four/SixReplace a whole jaw with extra implants for stability.
